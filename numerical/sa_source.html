Spalart-Allmaras source residual

After closure functions are known, the SA residual combines production, destruction, and a limited cross-diffusion term.

\[P = c_{b1}(1-f_{t2})\tilde S\,\tilde\nu\]
\[D = (c_{w1} f_w - c_{b1} f_{t2})\,\tilde\nu\, r_s\]
\[\mathrm{Diff} = \min\big(r_p P,\, (c_{b2}/\sigma)|\nabla\tilde\nu|^2/Re\big)\]
\[S = P - D + \mathrm{Diff}\]

Spectral contribution uses the negative part of linearized production-destruction rates: \(-\tfrac12(x-|x|)\).

References

  • Spalart, P. R. and Allmaras, S. R. A one-equation turbulence model.

  • NASA Turbulence Modeling Resource (SA).

  • See sa_closure.rst and references.rst.

Pure APIs: SaProduction, SaDestruction, SaDiffusion, SaLimitedDiffusion, SaSourceResidual, SaNegativePart. Tests: turb_sa_policy_test.
