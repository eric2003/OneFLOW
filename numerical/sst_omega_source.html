SST omega-equation production, dissipation, and cross-diffusion

The specific-dissipation equation closes SST / Wilcox / EASM-kw families with blended coefficients and an optional cross-diffusion term.

Blending

\[\phi = F_1\phi_1 + (1-F_1)\phi_2\]

Menter production / dissipation

\[P_\omega = \alpha\,\frac{\rho}{\mu_t} P_k Re, \qquad D_\omega = f_\beta\,\beta\,\rho\,\omega^2\]
\[CD_{k\omega} = 2(1-F_1)\rho\sigma_{\omega 2} (\nabla k\cdot\nabla\omega)/\omega\]

Wilcox-style production uses \(P_\omega = \alpha\,(\omega/k) P_k\) and \(CD = \sigma_d\rho\max(\nabla k\cdot\nabla\omega,0)/\omega\).

References

  • Menter, F. R. Two-equation eddy-viscosity turbulence models (SST).

  • Wilcox, D. C. Turbulence Modeling for CFD.

  • NASA Turbulence Modeling Resource — SST (https://turbmodels.larc.nasa.gov/sst.html).

  • See sst_k_source.rst and references.rst.
