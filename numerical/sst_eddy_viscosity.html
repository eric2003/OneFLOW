SST eddy viscosity and F2

\[\mathrm{arg}_2 = \max\Big( \frac{2\sqrt{k}}{\beta^* d\omega},\, \frac{500\nu}{\omega d^2 Re} \Big), \qquad F_2 = \tanh(\mathrm{arg}_2^2)\]
\[\mu_t = \frac{\rho k} {\max(\omega,\,\Omega F_2/a_1)}\,Re\]

with an optional laminar-ratio cap.

Intermediate checks: (F_2(0)=0), (F_2to 1) for large arg.

References

  • Menter SST; NASA TMR SST.

  • See sa_eddy_viscosity.rst and verification_layers.rst.

Pure APIs: SstF2Arg, SstF2FromArg, SstEddyViscosity.
