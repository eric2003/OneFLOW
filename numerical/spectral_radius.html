Face spectral radius and local time step

Local time stepping uses face inviscid and viscous spectral radii accumulated to cells. The pure formulas below are independent of mesh traversal.

Inviscid face spectral radius

\[\lambda_{\mathrm{inv}} = \tfrac12 A \big(|v_n| + c\big)\]

with face-midpoint relative normal velocity and ideal-gas sound speed.

Viscous estimates

Model 1 (momentum/energy diffusivity max):

\[\lambda_{\mathrm{vis}} = A^2 \cdot \frac{2\,\max\big(\tfrac43(\mu_l+\mu_t),\, \gamma(\mu_l/Pr_l+\mu_t/Pr_t)\big)} {Re\,\rho}\]

Model 2 uses face-to-cell distance; model 3 is a simplified \(A^2/(Re\rho)\) form.

Cell time step

\[\Delta t_{\mathrm{inv}} = \mathrm{CFL}\,\frac{V}{\lambda_{\mathrm{inv}}}, \qquad \Delta t \leftarrow \frac{\Delta t_{\mathrm{inv}}\,\Delta t_{\mathrm{vis}}} {\Delta t_{\mathrm{inv}}+\Delta t_{\mathrm{vis}}}\]

References

  • Blazek, J. Computational Fluid Dynamics: Principles and Applications (spectral-radius estimates for viscous CFL).

  • Toro, E. F. Riemann Solvers and Numerical Methods for Fluid Dynamics (wave speeds entering inviscid spectral radius).

  • OpenFOAM Programmer’s / User Guide (local time-stepping practice; compare implementation choices, not copy code).

Pure APIs: TimeStepPolicy.h. Tests: timestep_policy_test.

LUSGS reuse

UNsLusgs::CalcViscousTerm routes through the same viscous spectral models as local time stepping. The implicit face state uses Roe-weighted density, velocity, and total enthalpy. Its pressure is then reconstructed from that total enthalpy using the left-cell heat-capacity ratio, preserving the established LUSGS linearization. A weighted average of static pressure is not equivalent and must not replace this closure.
