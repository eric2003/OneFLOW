Stokes viscous stress and face flux

After face velocity gradients are known, the laminar viscous flux is built from the Stokes stress tensor and the heat-flux normal component.

Stress tensor

\[\tau_{ij} = \mu\Big(\partial_j u_i + \partial_i u_j\Big) - \tfrac{2}{3}\mu\,(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{v})\,\delta_{ij}\]

Momentum and energy face fluxes

\[\mathbf{F}_{\mathrm{mom}} = \boldsymbol{\tau}\cdot\mathbf{n}\]
\[F_E = \mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{F}_{\mathrm{mom}} + q_n\]

Pipeline

face velocity/temperature gradients
     |
     +--> StokesStressTensor (optional anisotropy add-on remains in caller)
     +--> ProjectStressMomentumFlux
     +--> ViscousEnergyFlux (+ heat normal)
     +--> DepositOrientedFaceFlux into residual

Pure APIs: StokesStressTensor, ProjectStressMomentumFlux, ViscousEnergyFlux (NsVisFluxPolicy.h). Tests: vis_flux_policy_test.

Nondimensional face scaling

\[\mathrm{coeff} = -\frac{1}{Re}\,A\]

so that stored face viscous fluxes match the residual convention DepositOrientedFaceFlux. Spectrum accumulation uses the same bilateral deposit helper DepositFaceScalarToCells.
