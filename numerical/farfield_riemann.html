Subsonic far-field Riemann invariants

For subsonic far-field faces, one-dimensional acoustic Riemann invariants along the face normal close the boundary state.

Invariants

\[R^+ = v_n^{\mathrm{in}} + \frac{2 c_{\mathrm{in}}}{\gamma-1}, \qquad R^- = v_n^{\mathrm{ref}} - \frac{2 c_{\mathrm{ref}}}{\gamma-1}\]
\[v_{n,b} = \tfrac12(R^+ + R^-), \qquad c_b = \tfrac14(R^+ - R^-)(\gamma-1)\]

Tangential velocity is taken from the upwind side (exit: interior; inlet: freestream). Density follows isentropic relation \(\rho = (c_b^2/(s\gamma))^{1/(\gamma-1)}\) and \(p = c_b^2\rho/\gamma\).

References

  • Blazek, J. Computational Fluid Dynamics: Principles and Applications.

  • Toro, E. F. Riemann Solvers and Numerical Methods for Fluid Dynamics.

  • See boundary_velocity.rst and references.rst.
